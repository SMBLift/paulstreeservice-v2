Who We Are

A Pittsburgh Family Business

Paul's Tree Service was founded in 1987 by Paul Gregorius with a simple mission: provide honest, reliable tree care at fair prices. What began as one man with a chainsaw and a pickup truck has grown into a full-service tree and property maintenance company serving the Greater Pittsburgh Area.

Now a 2nd generation family business, that same work ethic and commitment to quality is at the core of everything we do. We treat every property like our own, and every customer like a neighbor — because most of them are.

From removing a single hazardous branch to clearing an entire lot, no job is too big or too small. We bring the right equipment, the right crew, and decades of hands-on experience to every project.
